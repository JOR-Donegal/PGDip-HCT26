Week 9

In some iterations of this programme of study, Storage is a full 10 credit module. The work we do this week is to summarize the essentials, for students who are not doing a full 10 credit module.

Theory

I need to cover some simple storage fundamentals to begin. Next we will take a look at Storage Reliability, to understand some of the techniques we use.

There is only one effective way to study this. For each set of calculations I show, build a spreadsheet with notes and calculations. I will not ask you to present this, but it is an important thing to take from this module.
